# Mapaz Viral Growth Playbook
## Target: 10,000 Users in 30 Days

Based on proven strategies from Cluely ($120M valuation, 1B+ views), Nikita Bier's TBH/Gas (sold to Meta/Discord), BeReal, Locket, and Playkit's UGC campaigns.

---

## Core Philosophy

> "Attention is the new currency. You can have the best product, but if nobody knows about it, you have nothing."
> — Cluely Founders

**Be famous first, perfect second.** In the AI era, where apps can be cloned in weeks, speed and reach are more important than perfection. Distribution-first, product-second.

## Week-by-Week Execution Plan

### Week 1: Foundation & Content Machine Setup (Days 1-7)

#### Day 1-2: Define Your Viral Hook
Your hook needs to pass the **"15-Second Test"**: Can you explain mapaz's value in 15 seconds with just visuals?

**Potential hooks for mapaz:**
- "This AI knows what you want to eat better than you do"
- "Never eat at a mid restaurant again"
- "POV: You let AI pick your dinner spot"
- "I let an app read my mind and pick where I eat"
- "The app that knows you're lying when you say 'anything's fine'"

**Controversy angle (Cluely-style):** If half the audience doesn't hate it, it's not viral enough.
- "Google Maps is dead"
- "Yelp reviews are useless—here's why"
- "Stop asking your friends where to eat"

#### Day 3-4: Build Your Creator Army
Following the Cluely model (50 interns, 700 clippers, 200 videos/day):

**Minimum Viable Creator Team:**
- 10-15 micro-creators (5K-50K followers each)
- Focus on foodies, lifestyle, local guides, college students
- Pay structure: $20-40/video + $500-1,000 bonus for 500K+ views

**Where to recruit:**
- TikTok Creator Marketplace
- Instagram DMs to local food/lifestyle creators
- College campus job boards
- Fiverr/Upwork for video editors (clippers)
- Discord servers (food, local communities)

**Alternative: Playkit Model**
Hire 10+ "fractional ambassadors" posting 3x/day for 30 days = 900+ videos

#### Day 5-7: Content System Setup
**Video lengths that work:**
- 5-7 seconds: Quick reveals, transformations
- 27-35 seconds: Feature explanations, "watch me use this app"
- 55+ seconds: Full restaurant visit stories

**Mandatory elements:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- On-screen text immediately
- Strong problem statement or before/after
- Native TikTok aesthetic (NOT polished ads)

### Week 2: Hyper-Local Launch (Days 8-14)

#### The Nikita Bier Playbook
Don't launch everywhere. Launch in ONE hyper-specific community and dominate it.

**Target: One Neighborhood/College Campus**

**Selection criteria:**
- High density of restaurants/cafes
- Active social media scene
- College campus ideal (users see each other daily)
- Clear community identity (Brooklyn, Austin, Miami, etc.)

**Goal: 40% penetration in target area within 24 hours**

#### Guerrilla Instagram Strategy (TBH-style)
1. Create location-specific Instagram accounts:
   - @mapaz.brooklyn
   - @mapaz.austin
   - @mapaz.miami
   - etc.

2. Follow local foodies who identify their location in bios

3. Keep accounts **private** with teaser bio:
   > "You're invited to mapaz in [Location]. The AI that knows where you want to eat. Stay tuned."

4. At launch time (4PM local, when people think about dinner):
   - Switch to public
   - Update bio with App Store link
   - Accept all follow requests
   - Drop first viral video

#### College Ambassador Program (BeReal-style)
- Recruit 5-10 ambassadors per target campus
- Requirements: 1K+ followers minimum
- Pay: Free premium + $100/month + performance bonuses
- Task: 1 post/day showing app in action

### Week 3: Content Flood (Days 15-21)

#### The Cluely Math
> "If you make 100 videos, one will go viral. If you repost that viral video across 100 accounts, 20-30 will go viral too."

**Daily content targets:**
- 50+ unique videos across all creator accounts
- Repost winners to every platform immediately
- Track which hooks/formats perform

**Content categories to test:**

1. **"POV" videos**
   - POV: You ask AI where to eat and it actually understands you
   - POV: You're tired of asking "where should we eat"

2. **"Watch me" demonstrations**
   - Watch me let AI pick my dinner
   - I tried the app that reads your food preferences

3. **Reaction/Results videos**
   - The AI recommended THIS restaurant and I was shocked
   - Rating the spots my AI map app found

4. **Comparison/Controversy**
   - Google Maps vs Mapaz (which found better spots?)
   - I stopped using Yelp for this
   - Why I deleted Google Maps

5. **Lifestyle integration**
   - My new going-out routine
   - How I find date night spots now

#### Platform Distribution
Post EVERY video to:
- TikTok (primary)
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts
- X (Twitter) video
- Threads

**Timing:** Post between 4-8 PM local time (dinner decision hours)

---

### Week 4: Viral Amplification (Days 22-30)

#### Double Down on Winners
- Identify top 5 performing videos
- Create 20 variations of each winner
- Spread across all creator accounts
- Boost top performers with Spark Ads ($50-100/day)

#### Referral Loop Activation
**Dropbox increased signups 60% with referrals**

**Mapaz referral mechanics:**
- "Invite 3 friends, unlock premium recommendations"
- "Share your food profile, get personalized lists"
- "Create a group to decide where to eat together" (forces invites)

#### User-Generated Content Flywheel
Encourage users to share their:
- AI recommendation results
- "My mapaz knows me" moments
- Before/after of finding spots

**Branded hashtag:** #mapazknows or #mymapaz


### Video Script Template (30 seconds)

```
[0-3s] HOOK: "This app knows where you want to eat better than you do"
[3-8s] PROBLEM: "I'm so tired of scrolling through reviews and still picking wrong"
[8-18s] SOLUTION: "Mapaz uses AI that actually learns what you like—spicy, cozy, date-night vibes, whatever"
[18-25s] PROOF: *Show app in action, recommendation appearing*
[25-30s] CTA: "Link in bio, thank me later"
```

### Controversy Script Template

```
[0-3s] HOT TAKE: "Google Maps is lying to you"
[3-10s] EXPLAIN: "Those 'recommended' restaurants? Paid placements. Those reviews? Half are fake."
[10-20s] ALTERNATIVE: "I switched to this AI map that actually learns MY taste, not what advertisers want"
[20-25s] PROOF: *Show personal recommendation*
[25-30s] CHALLENGE: "Prove me wrong"
```

## Critical Success Factors

### 1. Speed Over Perfection
Ship fast. Iterate faster. The first version doesn't need to be perfect—it needs to be shareable.

### 2. Native Content Only
UGC that looks like ads fails. Content must feel like it belongs on TikTok, made by real users.

### 3. Hyper-Local First
Don't try to win everywhere. Win ONE neighborhood completely, then expand.

### 4. Controversy Gets Attention
Safe content gets ignored. Take a stance. "If half don't hate it, it's not viral enough."

### 5. Volume is Strategy
One video won't go viral. 100 might. 500 probably will. Cluely made 200/day.

### 6. Retention > Acquisition
10,000 users who churn = 0 users. Build sticky features:
- Daily personalized recommendation
- "Where should we eat?" group decision feature
- Food preference evolution tracking

## Platform-Specific Tactics

### TikTok
- Use trending sounds (check daily)
- Leverage TikTok Maps feature (location discovery)
- Post 3-5x/day per account
- Engage in comments (algorithm boost)

### Instagram Reels
- Cross-post TikTok content
- Use location tags aggressively
- Stories → Reels conversion
- Collab feature with local food accounts

### X (Twitter)
- Quote tweet foodie discourse
- "This app would solve this" replies
- Build in public updates
- Engage with local community accounts

### Reddit
- Genuine participation in r/foodie, r/[yourcity]
- Don't promote—be helpful first
- Answer "where should I eat" questions naturally
- Build karma before any app mentions

## Risk Mitigation

### If content isn't hitting:
- Test new hooks (not new creators)
- Analyze competitor viral content
- Shorten videos (try 7-15 seconds)
- Increase controversy angle

### If downloads don't convert to retention:
- Onboarding too slow (target: 3 seconds to value)
- First recommendation not impressive enough
- No reason to return daily

### If creators underperform:
- Switch to performance-only pay
- Provide more specific scripts
- Show examples of winning content
- Consider Playkit or agency model
