# SLIDE 18: Crisis Protocol

## The FIRE Method

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| **F - First Response** | Acknowledge within 1 hour. "We're aware. Investigating." |
| **I - Investigate** | Gather facts. Document timeline. |
| **R - Respond Fully** | Complete statement when you have facts. |
| **E - Execute & Update** | Follow through. Close the loop publicly. |

**Never go silent. Silence = guilt in crypto.**
